Abstractica comes with three game systems: **Ironsworn**, **Starforged**, and **Abstractica**,
a system-neutral sandbox for any game you play. You pick one under **Game System** when you
[create a campaign](/getting-started/first-campaign/#create-a-campaign), and the campaign keeps
it: you can't change a campaign's system later. Every system is in the free version.

More game systems are in development.

| Setting truths | — | 14 | — |

Ironsworn and Starforged figures are for the game on its own. With
[Abstractica Content](#abstractica-content), a campaign of either gets 4 more oracles, 65 more
tables, and 1 more generator. Abstractica's figures already include it.

How each tool works is in the User guide: [moves and dice](/guide/moves-and-dice/),
[oracles and tables](/guide/oracles-and-tables/), [generators](/guide/generators/),
[clocks and progress tracks](/guide/clocks-and-progress-tracks/), and [decks](/guide/decks/).

## Ironsworn

Shawn Tomkin's game of perilous quests in the dark-fantasy Ironlands, with its expansion
**Ironsworn: Delve** included.

- **Moves:** 48. The 35 core moves, in Adventure, Relationship, Combat, Suffer, Quest, and Fate,
  and Delve's 13, in Delve, Failure, Threat, and Rarity.
- **Oracles:** **Ask the Oracle**, for yes-or-no questions, and **Action and Theme**.
- **Tables:** 136. Delve's 103 include site themes and domains, dangers, features, denizens,
  threats, traps, and monstrosities. The other 33 include names, characters, locations,
  settlements, foes, and plot twists.
- **Assets:** 78: 37 paths, 17 rituals, 14 combat talents, and 10 companions.
- **Generators:** 8: **Delve Site**, **Denizen**, **Threat**, **Trap**, **Site Danger**, and
  **Anomaly** from Delve, and **Foe** and **Location**.
- **Character sheet:** Ironsworn's own. It can't be replaced with a custom sheet.

## Starforged

**Ironsworn: Starforged**, Shawn Tomkin's game of perilous exploration in the Forge, a galactic
frontier.

- **Moves:** 56, in Session, Adventure, Quest, Connection, Exploration, Combat, Suffer, Recover,
  Threshold, Legacy, Fate, and Scene Challenge.
- **Oracles:** **Ask the Oracle**, **Action + Theme**, **Descriptor + Focus**, and
  **Sector Name**.
- **Tables:** 256.
- **Assets:** 87: 46 paths, 15 modules, 11 companions, 7 deeds, 7 support vehicles, and
  1 command vehicle.
- **Setting truths:** 14, chosen in the **Truths** tab: Artificial Intelligence, Cataclysm,
  Communication and Data, Communities, Exodus, Horrors, Iron, Laws, Lifeforms, Magic, Medicine,
  Precursors, Religion, and War.
- **Generators:** 20: **Character**, **Creature**, **Derelict**, **Faction**, **Precursor
  Vault**, **Settlement**, **Settlement (sector survey)**, **Starship**, **Starting Sector**,
  and one for each of the 11 planet types.
- **Tension clocks:** **Begin the Scene** starts a scene challenge with a four-segment tension
  clock, which fills as you **Pay the Price** or concede a complication. Starforged has no
  **Clocks** tab.
- **Character sheet:** Starforged's own. It can't be replaced with a custom sheet.

## Abstractica

A system-neutral sandbox for whatever game you're playing, or for playing without one. It has
Abstractica's own moves and tools, and always includes [Abstractica Content](#abstractica-content).

- **Moves:** 18, in Checks, Story, Tracks, Recover, and Session. The four checks each resolve a
  different way: **Make a Check** with the classic action roll (an action die against two
  challenge dice), **Risk It** on 2d6, **Test Fate** with four Fate dice against a difficulty,
  and **Beat the Odds** with a d20 against a target number.
- **Oracles:** 5: **Ask the Oracle**, for yes-or-no questions, and the four
  [themed oracles](#abstractica-content).
- **Tables:** 91: Abstractica's 4 (**Complication**, **Event Focus**, **Scene Twist**, and
  **Weather**) and all 87 from Abstractica Content.
- **Generators & Procedures:** the **Town** generator; the procedures **Explore a Site**,
  **Frame a Scene**, **Make Camp**, and **Travel Leg**; and the **Expedition** loop, which chains
  travel, a scene, and what you do about it.
- **Clocks and timers:** threat clocks, which can ask the oracle whether they tick, and
  countdowns you fill by hand, with 4, 6, 8, 10, or 12 segments; and timers that count down in
  real minutes. They have their own **Clocks** tab, which Ironsworn and Starforged campaigns
  don't.
- **Card decks:** 4: **Standard Playing Cards** (52 cards), **Rider-Waite Tarot** (78),
  **Major Arcana** (22), and **Minor Arcana** (56).
- **Progress tracks** ranked Minor, Moderate, Major, Grand, and Legendary.
- **Character sheet:** the default sheet has five approaches (Force, Finesse, Focus, Flair, and
  Guile); Health, Resolve, and Resources meters; Luck; a concept; traits and gear; and
  conditions. You can replace it with your own.

### Custom character sheets

In an Abstractica campaign, the character sheet is yours to design. Choose or build one with
**Character sheet** when you create the campaign, or later in **Campaign settings** →
**Character sheet**, with **Edit sheet…** and **Switch to…**.

Each campaign keeps its own copy of its sheet, so editing it never changes another campaign.
Sheets you build when creating a campaign, or keep with **Save to my sheets**, go into your
library for later campaigns. How sheets are put together is in the
[System authoring guide](/authoring/overview/).

## Abstractica Content

Abstractica's own oracles and tables, written to work with any game. Every Abstractica campaign
has them. In Ironsworn and Starforged they're optional: **Include Abstractica Content** on the
new-campaign form is on by default, and you can turn it on or off at any time in
**Campaign settings** → **Content**. Turning it off keeps everything you made with it.

It adds:

- **The themed oracles:** **Fate**, **Story**, **The World**, and **The Unseen**. They answer in
  the campaign's oracle theme. Most of the oracles that moves suggest under **Ask** are these, so
  in Ironsworn and Starforged those suggestions appear when the campaign includes Abstractica
  Content.
- **Tables:** Chronicle tables, Spark Tables, settlement tables, and the tables behind the
  themed oracles.
- **The Town generator**, which makes a settlement with its size, name, population, trouble,
  notable feature, and notable folk.

The content holds 87 tables: 43 that suit any genre, 22 fantasy, and 22 science fiction. A
campaign gets the ones that suit its game: 65 in Ironsworn (any genre and fantasy), 65 in
Starforged (any genre and science fiction), and all 87 in Abstractica. **Campaign settings**
counts all 87 whichever system you play.

### Oracle themes

The oracle theme is the genre the themed oracles answer in. The same roll means the same thing
in every theme; only the telling changes. You choose a theme when you create a campaign and can
change it in **Campaign settings** → **Oracle theme**; either place offers the choice only when
the campaign includes Abstractica Content. Results already in your journal keep their wording.

| Theme | Offered in | As Campaign settings describes it |
|---|---|---|
| Low Fantasy | Ironsworn (the default), Abstractica (the default) | Mud, iron and hearth. Magic is a rumour; winter is not. |
| High Fantasy | Ironsworn, Abstractica | Ages, orders and open magic — mythic in scale, baroque in its workings. |
| Space Opera | Starforged (the default), Abstractica | Many worlds, a ship and a crew — pulp adventure on a lived-in frontier, under great houses. |
| Cyberpunk | Starforged, Abstractica | One city, a body and a contract. Noir streets under corporate weather. |
