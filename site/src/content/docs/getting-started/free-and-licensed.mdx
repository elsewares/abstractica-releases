Abstractica is free to use with one campaign. A license removes that limit. Nothing else
differs: every game system, tool, and oracle is in the free version.

## The free version

- One campaign, with as many sessions as you like.
- No account, no time limit, no trial period.

When you have one campaign, the **Begin New Campaign** button on the campaign list is replaced
by *The free version supports one campaign*, with **Buy a license →** and
**Enter a license key**.

Deleting your campaign frees the slot, but deletion is permanent — the campaign and everything
in it is gone.

## What a license gets you

- Unlimited campaigns.
- Use on **2 computers** at once.
- A one-time purchase — no subscription.

Buy one from **Buy a license →** in the app, or from
[Polar's checkout](https://buy.polar.sh/polar_cl_h6pAT8oXy3Z7b7ayAzWNFyBzzDKmAXTPOzOpW2dFPGl).
Polar handles the payment and emails your key; the price is shown at checkout and on
[abstractica.io](https://abstractica.io). Refunds are available within 14 days of purchase —
see the [EULA](https://github.com/elsewares/abstractica-releases/blob/main/EULA.md).

## Activating your license

You need an internet connection to activate.

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings**: Abstractica menu → **Settings…** (⌘,) on macOS; **File** → **Settings…**
   (Ctrl+,) on Windows and Linux. From the campaign list you can also click **Enter a license key**.
2. In the **License** section, paste your key. It looks like `ABST-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`.
3. Click **Activate**.

</Steps>

You'll see *License activated. Thank you!*, and the License section then reads
*This device is licensed*, followed by the last characters of your key. Your existing campaign
stays as it was; you can now create more.

If activation fails, the message under the key field says why. See
[Troubleshooting → License problems](/help/troubleshooting/#license-problems).

## Playing offline

Once activated, Abstractica doesn't need the internet to run. It re-checks your license when
it starts, if the last successful check was more than 7 days ago and you're online.

If it can't reach the licensing service for a long stretch:

- Your license stays valid for **30 days** after the last successful check.
- Then comes a **14-day grace period**. Abstractica shows *Reconnect to the internet to reverify
  your license* at startup and in Settings, and everything still works.
- After that, Abstractica treats this computer as free: your campaigns stay and remain editable,
  but you can't create new ones. Your key is kept, so going back online and choosing
  **Reactivate** in Settings restores the license.

## Moving to another computer

Your license follows the computer, not your files. Copying your `.abstractica` folder moves your
campaigns but not your license.

<Steps>

1. On the old computer, open **Settings** → **License** and click **Deactivate on this device**.
2. On the new computer, install Abstractica and [activate](#activating-your-license) with the same key.

</Steps>

If the old computer is lost or broken, release it from the
[Polar customer portal](https://polar.sh/elsewares/portal): sign in with the email you bought
with and deactivate the old device there.

To move your campaigns too, see the [FAQ](/help/faq/#how-do-i-move-to-a-new-computer).

## Lost your key

Sign in to the [Polar customer portal](https://polar.sh/elsewares/portal) with the email you
bought with; your key is listed there. For anything else about purchases, email
**hello@elsewares.io**.
